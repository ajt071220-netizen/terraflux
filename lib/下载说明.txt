Three.js 本地库文件清单（已全部就位，无需再下载）
=====================================================

本目录需要以下 3 个文件（新版 three.js 拆分为两个文件，缺一不可）：

  lib\three.module.js                      ← build/three.module.js
  lib\three.core.js                        ← build/three.core.js（module 的实际依赖）
  lib\addons\controls\OrbitControls.js     ← examples/jsm/controls/OrbitControls.js

下载源（任选一个，浏览器直接打开另存即可）：

  淘宝镜像：
    https://registry.npmmirror.com/three/latest/files/build/three.module.js
    https://registry.npmmirror.com/three/latest/files/build/three.core.js
    https://registry.npmmirror.com/three/latest/files/examples/jsm/controls/OrbitControls.js

  jsDelivr：
    https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@latest/build/three.module.js
    https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@latest/build/three.core.js
    https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@latest/examples/jsm/controls/OrbitControls.js
